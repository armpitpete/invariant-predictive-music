# Music-Formula Project — Final Result v1.0

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** **PROJECT RESULT FROZEN — THIS RESEARCH PHASE IS COMPLETE**  
**Branch purpose:** close the music-formula investigation at the strongest conclusion actually supported by its frozen evidence. This file does not introduce a new score, retune A5, reopen blocked validation, or authorise listener recruitment.

## 1. Final answer

The project has **not** discovered a defensible universal scalar formula for "good music".

It has produced a narrower and more useful result:

> **Music appears to derive part of its effect from a learnable identity that supports prediction. An event can gain musical force when it changes or resolves expectation without destroying that learned identity, and when what follows integrates or makes use of the change.**

This is the project's final mechanism-level result. It is intentionally not presented as a universal law of preference, beauty, emotional impact, memorability, groove, originality, cultural meaning, or commercial success.

In compact form:

\[
\text{learnable identity}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction}
\rightarrow
\text{informative deviation or resolution}
\rightarrow
\text{identity preservation}
\rightarrow
\text{later integration}
\]

The early idea that one coherence-like number might itself be the music formula did not survive hostile testing. The useful result came from separating structural identity from predictive dynamics and from human perceptual consequence.

## 2. What A5 is

A5 is retained as the project's **structural identity/coherence detector**, not as a musical-quality score.

Its frozen top-level form is:

\[
A_5(t)=[M(t)Q(t)G O]^{1/3}
\]

with persistent fabric `F = G × O`, where the conceptual roles are:

- `M` — multi-scale recurrence / identity;
- `Q` — local embedded-event quality;
- `G` — persistent cross-stream accountability;
- `O` — ordered relational-state continuity.

The important advance over the failed early measures is that A5 does not allow one repetitive stream, unordered marginals, or intact individual streams to stand in for the relationships among streams and their order through time.

### Evidence earned by A5

A5 passed its frozen E1–E7 development hostile suite.

It then passed the independently frozen Axis-S structural validation against all three held-out corruption families:

1. joint 5-beat block shuffle;
2. joint 7-beat block shuffle;
3. joint variable 5–9-beat segment shuffle.

For every family:

- final A5 discrimination: **PASS**;
- persistent-order `O` discrimination: **PASS**;
- generalized artificial-join defence: **PASS**.

The frozen randomisation tests gave `p = 0.009901` for the final-A5 and persistent-`O` comparisons, with the required song-direction counts met. Artificial joins received no positive premium.

That is real evidence that A5 detects non-trivial musical organisation in the preserved development material rather than merely rewarding smooth marginals, repetition, or splice artefacts.

### Evidence A5 has not earned

The full validation firewall remains **INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED** because Axis U cannot legitimately run without the original development-equivalent audio-to-28-feature extraction pipeline.

The recovery record establishes:

- original extractor provenance is incomplete;
- development equivalence has not passed the frozen thresholds;
- no unseen validation song has been processed under an authorised substitute pipeline;
- A5 has not been changed to escape this block.

Therefore A5 must **not** be described as a generally validated unseen-song audio metric.

The blocked Axis U is preserved as provenance debt, not repaired by silently reconstructing a plausible extractor.

## 3. What IPM contributes

IPM supplies the dynamic variables that structural identity alone cannot provide. The present engine exposes, among other quantities:

- predictive probability `P(x)`;
- surprise `S(x) = -log2 P(x)`;
- invariant similarity;
- retrospective coherence;
- retrospective necessity;
- the current heuristic `ipm_score`.

These variables provide experimental machinery for studying prediction, surprise, belonging and later justification.

They do **not** establish that the engine's probability distribution is the listener's probability distribution. Current IPM expectation is a model/design prior, not a calibrated measurement of human expectation.

Therefore:

> **`ipm_score` is not "the music formula" and must not be promoted as one.**

No new scalar weights are authorised by this closeout.

## 4. The three-layer result

The project is best understood as three distinct layers.

### Layer 1 — structural identity

Question: can the system maintain a learnable musical world whose recurrence, ordering and cross-stream relationships survive meaningful transformation but respond to structural damage?

Current evidence: **partially supported by A5** — development suite PASS and Axis S PASS; unseen-song Axis U blocked.

### Layer 2 — predictive-information dynamics

Question: how does an event change expectation, uncertainty and the model of what may follow while remaining recognisably inside the learned musical world?

Current evidence: **mechanistically implemented and testable in IPM**, but its probabilities remain model expectations rather than measured listener expectations.

### Layer 3 — human perceptual consequence

Question: do people actually experience identity-preserving, prediction-changing and later-integrated events as more coherent, retrospectively meaningful, memorable, desirable to hear again, or preferred?

Current evidence: **not established**. No real participant result exists.

The existing Counterfactual Episode v2 study reached external-listener technical/governance readiness, but the explicit recruitment decision remains **HOLD — DO NOT BEGIN THE 36-LISTENER PILOT**.

## 5. What the project supports

The evidence supports these claims at the stated scope:

1. Musical structural identity cannot safely be reduced to repetition inside independent streams.
2. Cross-stream relationships and temporal order matter to a useful structural measure.
3. Musical recurrence must be considered at more than one timescale.
4. A5 is a viable structural-coherence/identity representation on its frozen development suite and held-out Axis-S reorderings.
5. Coherence alone is not a demonstrated measure of musical value.
6. Surprise alone is not a demonstrated measure of musical value.
7. A plausible next-level mechanism is relational: expectation matters in the context of learned identity and of what the music subsequently does with an event.
8. The exact uncertainty/surprise utility surface is contextual and must be measured rather than assumed as one universal optimum.

---

## 6. What the project does not support

The following claims are explicitly rejected or unearned:

- a universal numerical formula for good music;
- universal numerical weights for identity, surprise or integration;
- "higher A5 means better music";
- "higher `ipm_score` means humans will prefer the music";
- full A5 unseen-song audio validation;
- equivalence between IPM probabilities and listener expectations;
- listener preference for IPM-generated material;
- a claim that retrospective sense is equivalent to beauty, liking, emotion, groove, originality or cultural value.

These exclusions are part of the result, not loose ends to be hidden.

---

## 7. Final project decisions

The following decisions close this research phase:

1. **Freeze A5.** Do not tune it against the blocked Axis U or against later listener outcomes.
2. **Do not create A6/A7 merely to chase a better scalar.** A successor representation requires a genuinely new falsification problem, not score optimisation.
3. **Keep Axis U blocked** unless the original extractor is recovered or a reconstruction independently passes the already-frozen development-equivalence thresholds.
4. **Keep `ipm_score` classified as an engineering heuristic.** Do not rename it as a perceptual or musical law.
5. **Keep the recruitment HOLD in force.** This closeout does not contact, enrol or collect data from participants.
6. **Preserve the proposed 2 × 2 identity × future-integration experiment as successor research**, not as a condition for declaring this phase complete.
7. **Treat a universal scalar music formula as unsupported at the present evidence level.** The project result is the mechanism-level factorisation above.

## 8. Optional successor falsification

If the project is reopened for new science rather than scalar tuning, the highest-value experiment remains the separately designed 2 × 2 test:

- HIGH vs LOW local identity continuity;
- INTEGRATED vs UNINTEGRATED future material.

That experiment can ask whether **belonging** and **what comes later** make independent causal contributions to retrospective sense, with human surprise/expectation manipulation checks and tightly matched suffixes.

It is a successor study. It is not missing work required to close this investigation.

---

## 9. Final verdict

**MUSIC-FORMULA PROJECT: COMPLETE AT MECHANISM LEVEL.**

The original ambition of finding one robust scalar that explains musical value has not been supported and should not be cosmetically rescued.

The durable result is instead:

> **Musical structure appears to work through relationships: a learnable identity creates expectations; events can alter or resolve those expectations while remaining inside that identity; and later context can make those events useful. Structural coherence can be measured separately from the human value that may arise from this process.**

A5 is retained as the current structural substrate with clearly bounded validation. IPM is retained as the predictive experimental machinery. Human consequence remains a separate empirical question.

That is the finished result of this research phase.
