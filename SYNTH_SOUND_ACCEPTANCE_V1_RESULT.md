# Synth Sound Acceptance v1 — Result

Status: **FAIL**

Owner judgment recorded 19 August 2026.

Frozen gate question:

> Does Machine Synth Engine v1 sound like a real musical instrument rather than prototype/test audio?

Owner response:

> Fail: it sounds uncomfortable, toyish, flat, basic

## Scope of the finding

The judgment applies to the frozen four-file audition set as a whole rather than to one isolated timestamp or lane:

1. Tune solo
2. Bass solo
3. Rhythm solo
4. Full mix

The audition source and synthesis contract remain frozen as evidence. This result does **not** justify changing the IPM composition engine, Tune scoring, musical seed, listener-study renderer, or recruitment state.

## What failed

Machine Synth Engine v1 passed its technical implementation checks but failed audible product acceptance. Therefore technical properties such as deterministic rendering, stereo PCM, independent lane presets, envelopes, filtering, panning and ambience are not sufficient evidence of a credible instrument sound.

The reported failure dimensions are:

- **uncomfortable** — the sound is not pleasant/comfortable enough for sustained musical use;
- **toyish** — timbres read as prototype/demo synthesis rather than convincing instruments;
- **flat** — insufficient depth, dimensionality, articulation and/or mix-space differentiation;
- **basic** — insufficient timbral richness and expressive character.

These are whole-render acceptance findings. They must not be reinterpreted as a request to patch one timestamp or cherry-pick a different seed.

## Consequence

**Machine Use Gate remains BLOCKED.**

Do not evaluate NEW / ACTIVITY / SURPRISE / HOLD / PLAY / FINISH as a musical interaction while Synth Sound Acceptance is failed. A poor renderer would confound the product judgment.

## Protected next gate

Before implementing another synth, freeze **Machine Synth Replacement Contract v2** against these four failure dimensions.

The replacement contract must define, before new audition results are heard, what must be demonstrably different in:

- tonal comfort;
- timbral credibility;
- dynamic/articulative depth;
- spatial/mix depth;
- timbral richness/character;
- full-mix integration.

No IPM composition changes are authorised by this failure. No listener recruitment is authorised.
