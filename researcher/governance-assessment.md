## Data-protection classification

Returned P### exports are pseudonymous personal data while the separately held invitation/mail linkage exists. Email transport metadata is personal data but is kept out of the research response table. No special-category or criminal-offence data is intentionally collected.

Working Article 6 basis for an independent/private-sector controller: **legitimate interests**. Participation consent remains the ethical agreement to take part rather than the Article 6 lawful basis.

Article 89/DPA 2018 safeguards implemented: minimisation, pseudonymisation at source, separation of contact linkage, least-privilege access, bounded retention, no individual measures/decisions, and no behavioural advertising or profiling.

## DPIA screening

A mandatory DPIA is not indicated on the frozen scope. The study does not perform systematic/extensive profiling with significant effects, large-scale special-category/criminal-offence processing, systematic public monitoring, automated denial of service/opportunity, biometric/genetic processing, large-scale data matching, covert/invisible collection, vulnerable-subject targeting or processing whose breach could create physical-safety risk.

The only automated integrity processing verifies schedule/WAV/session provenance and does not make consequential decisions about a person. The participant population is restricted to adults able to consent for themselves.

Re-screen for a DPIA before any scope change involving vulnerable participants, sensitive data, external data matching, profiling, automated consequential decisions, large-scale expansion or new tracking/analytics.

## Ethics / HRA screening

The frozen project is non-clinical music-perception research outside NHS services. It does not involve medicines/devices, human tissue, NHS patient records, adults unable to consent or the categories of entirely non-NHS studies for which the HRA identifies NHS REC review as still required.

Therefore NHS HRA Approval / NHS REC review is **not the applicable route on the current scope**. If an employer, university, funder or other institution governs the research, its own ethics/governance rules must be followed instead. If there is no governing institution, record the project as independent and retain this proportionate risk/ethics assessment with the study record.

## ICO fee screening

The actual controller must complete the ICO data-protection-fee self-assessment before recruitment. Research is not itself a listed fee exemption. The household exemption depends on the activity being genuinely personal/family/household with no commercial or professional connection; the not-for-profit exemption is narrow and does not create a general research exemption.

Do not infer exemption from the small sample, absence of profit or independent status. Record the self-assessment's exact outcome. If a fee-paying micro controller falls in tier one, the current standard fee is £52, reduced by £5 for direct debit.
